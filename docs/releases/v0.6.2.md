A small bug-fix release on top of [v0.6.1](https://github.com/cyryllo/ArchFlow/releases/tag/v0.6.1) (new editor, new icon set, desktop downloads).

### 🐛 Fixes

- **Labels no longer hide the top of tall icons.** Node labels used to sit at a fixed height, so some of the new, taller icons poked up behind them. The label is now always placed above the icon.
- **Clicking the middle of a node selects it.** The dotted line from a label to its node used to catch clicks right in the centre of the icon.
- **The right-click menu opens immediately.** Before, it sometimes only appeared after you moved the mouse.
