A small fix release on top of [v0.6.2](https://github.com/cyryllo/ArchFlow/releases/tag/v0.6.2).

### 🐛 Fixes

- **Settings are remembered.** Tool letters, *Zoom to cursor* and the connector mode (click or drag) used to go back to their defaults after a reload, and even after switching the language. Now they're kept, like the mouse panning options.
- **"Share" only where it works.** The Share button in *My diagrams* is shown only when diagrams are stored on the server (Docker). In the desktop app and in browser-only mode it created links nobody else could open.

### 📖 Documentation

New since v0.6.2: a [quick start](https://github.com/cyryllo/ArchFlow/blob/master/docs/quick-start.md) and a full [user manual](https://github.com/cyryllo/ArchFlow/blob/master/docs/manual.md), both in English and Polish, with screenshots.
